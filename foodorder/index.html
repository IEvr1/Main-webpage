Online Food Orders, Delivery & Kitchen — with SMS confirmation

Customers order from a bilingual menu, confirm via SMS, and track their order. Owners manage the kitchen live — no customer accounts required. For businesses in European countries.

  • Phone-only orders, errors & waiting Structured online menu and checkout in a few steps — no hold music, no misheard items.
  • Customers don't know order status SMS with a manage link — cancel, modify, or view history without calling back.
  • Delivery zone uncertainty Google Maps pin and radius validation — customers know upfront if delivery is available.
  • Kitchen chaos & driver coordination Live owner dashboard plus a separate driver board with SMS updates to customers.

Frequently asked questions

Do customers need to register?

No. Customers order from a bilingual menu and receive an SMS with a manage link — no accounts, no passwords.

Is it ready for European countries?

Yes. It supports per-country settings (VAT, timezone), European mobile numbers, and a multilingual interface.

How long does setup take?

Each restaurant gets its own deployment with full data isolation. Setup typically takes under an hour once your menu is ready.

Does it support both takeaway and delivery?

Yes. Customers can choose pickup or delivery, with Google Maps pin validation for delivery zones, a kitchen dashboard, and a separate driver board.
