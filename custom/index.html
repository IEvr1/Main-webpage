Frequently asked questions

How is this different from your ready-made products?

Ready-made products (bookings, food orders, shop traffic) cover specific needs. Custom is for unique requirements that don't fit those templates.

How much does a custom app cost?

It depends on complexity. After our discussion we provide a quote with a clear cost range — no commitment at the start.

How long does development take?

Simple apps may be ready in a few weeks. Larger projects take longer — the timeline is agreed before we start.

How do I get started?

Send us a message via the contact form or email contact@nexaipla.com. Describe your business and goals — we reply within 48 hours.
