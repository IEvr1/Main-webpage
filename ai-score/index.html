Eighteen structured questions. A clear 0–100 score, a traffic-light band, and the inefficiencies holding AI back in your business. For businesses in Cyprus and Greece.

Frequently asked questions

Is this assessment free?

Yes. The 18-question assessment is free, runs in your browser, and shows your score instantly — no signup required. You can take it in any of the supported languages from the dropdown.

How is the score calculated?

Each of the 18 answers scores 0–3 points across six dimensions (max 54). Your total is converted to a 0–100 score and mapped to a traffic-light band: red (0–39), amber (40–69), green (70–100).

What happens after I see my score?

You can request a custom AI solution. We prefill the contact form with your score and weakest areas so we can discuss a tailored next step.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Scoring stays in your browser. We only receive your answers if you choose to send a contact message with your score summary.
