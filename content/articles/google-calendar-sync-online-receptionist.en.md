**Short answer:** Yes. [Online Receptionist 24/7](https://www.nexaipla.com/onlinebooking/) syncs with Google Calendar per team member. When a customer books with a particular person, the appointment appears in that person's calendar, and when it is cancelled or its time is changed, the calendar is updated. Everyone sees their schedule on their own phone, and the shared board shows the whole shop. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

In a salon of four people, the question "what do I have tomorrow?" is heard ten times a day. The answer is in one place: the notebook or the reception computer. Whoever has a day off, whoever starts later, whoever is already at home, has to ask somebody.

## Two calendars that do not agree

Most professionals already have a personal calendar on their phone. The dentist is there, the school party, the car inspection. Work appointments are elsewhere.

Two calendars mean that the picture of the day exists nowhere in full. Some copy appointments into their phone by hand. They do it until the first change they did not get round to entering.

## What "per person" means

The sync is not with one shared calendar for the whole shop. It is with each person's calendar.

Maria sees Maria's appointments in her Google Calendar. Andreas sees Andreas's. Nobody has to hunt for their own lines among forty that belong to others.

That fits the way booking is done. The customer chooses a service and a person. The appointment belongs to one human being from the first moment.

## What happens when an appointment is booked

The customer completes the booking at eleven at night. They get their SMS. The appointment appears on the shop's board and, a little later, in the calendar of the person they chose.

In the morning that person opens their phone and sees that the day has one more appointment. Nobody phoned them.

## What happens when it changes

The customer changes the time from the SMS link, or cancels. The board updates, and the calendar with it.

This is where copying by hand always fails. The appointment gets written, but the cancellation does not get erased. With the sync, the calendar follows.

## The board remains the source

It is worth being clear about who does what. The availability customers see is calculated by the booking system, live, by service and person. The shared board shows all appointments, the figures, and the closures.

Google Calendar is each person's window onto their own part. It is there so that one can see the day without opening anything else.

That is why changes concerning the shop are made on the board. When someone will be away, you declare the closure there, so that customers do not see free times that do not exist. And if someone falls ill suddenly, you use the board's emergency reschedule, which looks after the customers too.

## What is needed from each team member

A Google account. Most already have one, from their phone.

Connecting their calendar to the system, once, at setup.

And a decision: personal or work account? Many prefer a separate calendar for work inside the same account, so that they can hide it on holiday. That is each person's choice.

## What the owner sees

The shared board, with the whole team. No access to anybody's personal calendar is needed. Staff keep private what is private, and the shop sees what belongs to the shop.

## When someone leaves or joins

Teams change. When a new member joins, they are added with their services and connect their calendar. When someone leaves, they are removed, and customers stop seeing them as an option.

At every such change a check of the board's access links is also worthwhile: who has them.

## Small businesses with one person

The sync is just as useful when the "team" is you. The physiotherapist who works alone sees in the same calendar Tuesday's patients and their child's piano lesson. There is no need to look in two places to work out whether there is time.

## What to check in the first week

Book a test appointment with each person and make sure it appeared in the right calendar. Change its time from the link and see that it moved. Cancel it and see that it went.

Three tests per person, five minutes each. After that you know you can put the notebook away.

## What the team gains

- Everyone sees their schedule on their own phone
- Changes and cancellations arrive without a phone call
- Nobody copies appointments by hand
- The owner has the overall picture on the board

## How you set it up

1. List the team members and each one's services.
2. Ask each which Google account they want to use.
3. Connect each person's calendar at setup.
4. Run three tests per person: booking, change, cancellation.
5. Agree that leave and days off are declared as closures on the board.
