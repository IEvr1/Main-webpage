**Short answer:** It depends on whether your need is common or particular. If it is appointments, food orders, live shop traffic, questions over documents, or all-day school meals, there is a ready-made NexAIpla app that starts quickly. If the task is yours alone, you describe it to [Custom AI Tools](https://www.nexaipla.com/ai/) and within 48 hours learn whether it can be done, roughly what it costs, and in how much time. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

It is the same decision as the suit. The off-the-peg one you wear today and it costs less. The tailored one fits exactly, but you wait and pay the tailor. Neither is "better". The question is whether your body fits the ready sizes.

## The ready-made applications, and what each does

[Online Receptionist 24/7](https://www.nexaipla.com/onlinebooking/) books appointments by chat, QR code, and SMS, with no customer account, with a sync to each person's Google Calendar. It does not answer phone calls.

[Online Food Orders](https://www.nexaipla.com/foodorder/) takes food orders with a bilingual menu, no accounts, a kitchen screen, a driver board, and a delivery zone on a map.

[Live Business Traffic](https://www.nexaipla.com/shoptraffic/) shows customers how busy the shop is right now. It is for walk-in businesses, and does not book appointments.

[DocsApp](https://www.nexaipla.com/docsapp/) answers questions over your documents, offline, on a Windows computer. It is not an email management tool.

[All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/) is for parents' associations and schools: absence notices from parents, live portions for the kitchen, a monthly menu.

## When ready-made fits

When your need is described by one of the sentences above, with no "yes, but".

When you want it soon. A ready-made application has already solved the problems you have not thought of yet.

When you do not want to pay for the building. The cost of development has been shared among many.

When you want to see first and decide afterwards. The ready-made thing exists and you can look at it.

## When the bespoke is needed

When the task is specific to your own business. The way you cost jobs. The form of the orders you receive. The report you prepare every Monday.

When it must connect to programs you already have, and do not intend to change.

When no ready-made application does it. Sorting incoming email is such an example, and we examine it in [I cannot keep up with my email](/en/articles/cannot-keep-up-with-emails).

When you have confidentiality requirements that determine where the tool will run.

## The "almost" test

Often a ready-made application covers most of what you want, and a remainder is left.

Look at the remainder honestly. Is it a need or a habit? "We have always written the customer's name in capitals" is a habit. "The law requires us to keep this item" is a need.

If the remainder is habit, it is usually cheaper to change the habit than to build around it.

## Signs that you are squeezing a ready-made tool

You use fields for things they were not made for.

You keep a spreadsheet alongside "for what does not fit".

You ask it for something it states plainly that it does not do. An appointment app to pick up the phone. A live traffic page to book appointments. A document tool to manage correspondence. A meals app to collect money.

If you recognise two of these, your need is probably particular.

## Both together

You need not choose one for the whole business.

A medical office can use the ready-made application for appointments, and a bespoke tool for something that concerns it alone. The common part gets the common solution. The particular part gets the particular one.

Often that is the most economical combination: you pay for building only what really does not exist.

## Time and cost

With the ready-made application, the time is the setup: entering your services, your menu, your team.

With the bespoke tool, the feasibility reply comes first, with a percentage, an indicative cost range, a time estimate, and recommended steps. From there you decide whether it is worth it.

In both cases, the largest cost is often your own time. In the first it is smaller.

## If you are not sure

Describe the need anyway. You need not have decided yourself which category it belongs to.

The reply's recommended next steps may very well be "a ready-made application already does this". We have no reason to build you something that exists.

## Three decisions

1. A hair salon wants customers to book by themselves. The need is common, and the ready-made appointment application is enough.
2. A window-frame company wants an automatic quote from dimensions and materials, with its own price list. No ready-made application does that, and it describes the need for a bespoke tool.
3. A restaurant uses the ready-made ordering application and asks about a bespoke tool only for its orders to suppliers.

## What you gain by choosing correctly

- You do not pay for building something that exists ready-made
- You do not squeeze your work into a tool that does not suit it
- You start quickly where the need is common
- You get an opinion within 48 hours where the need is particular

1. Write your need in one sentence.

4. If it does not fit, describe it in the form or by voice message.
