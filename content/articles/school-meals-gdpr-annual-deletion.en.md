> **Quick answer:** In [All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/) personal data is deleted automatically on 31 July every year. Until then the system keeps only what the meal service needs: name, class, parents' phone numbers, allergies, and absences. Personal links expire on the same day, staff enter with a secret link and a PIN, and a privacy policy is published on the school's page. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

A parents' association that organises school meals holds sensitive information whether it wants to or not: which child is in which class, which mobile number belongs to which family, which child must not eat nuts. Usually this lives in a spreadsheet on a volunteer's personal computer. When the volunteer changes, the file is forwarded by email. Three years later nobody knows how many copies exist, or why they still exist.

## What the system keeps, and why

It keeps five things: the child's name, the class, the parents' phone numbers, the allergies or foods to avoid that the parent wrote, and the absences from meals. Each has a job. Name and class produce the portions for each class. The phone number sends the personal link and identifies the parent on the absence form. Allergies reach the kitchen screen. Absences are the portion count itself.

The purpose is the meal service, the absences, and communication about them. No addresses, identity numbers, or medical history are asked for. The page has no advertising cookies.

## The 31st of July

A school year has a natural end, and the data follows it. On 31 July each year personal data is deleted automatically. Nobody has to remember to do it in the middle of the summer.

On the same date the parents' personal links and the staff sessions expire. An SMS from last October, forgotten on an old phone, opens nothing in August.

The association also has a manual option. The admin screen has a deletion of the school year's data, which asks for explicit confirmation before it runs, because it cannot be undone.

## In September you start from a clean list

The new year does not inherit the old one. The association imports the new list of children, with the classes as they now are and the phone numbers as parents gave them this year, and sends fresh personal links. Children who have left the school exist nowhere, without anyone having to find and delete them one by one.

Parents write the allergies again at the start of the year. That is a small job, and it is right that it is done: an allergy from last September may no longer apply, or another may have been added.

## Who sees what while the year lasts

The data is not visible to everyone who has a role. A parent sees only their own child, from the personal link. The kitchen sees the day's portions, allergies, and comments, not phone numbers. The office searches for children in order to register absences. The association's admin role holds the list. Staff enter with a secret link and a PIN, and after the PIN the link's key does not stay in the address bar.

The public absence form is designed so that it does not reveal whether a mobile number is on the list. Someone trying random numbers does not learn which families have a child at the school.

## What you tell parents

The footer of the page has "Privacy", with the policy in plain language: who the controller is, for what purpose, what is kept, until when, and how someone exercises their rights. The controller is the body that organises the meals, usually the parents' association, and requests for access or deletion go through it.

This article describes how the product behaves. It is not legal advice. The association remains responsible for its own obligations, for example for what it does with lists it keeps outside the system.

## Three moments in the year

1. In September a parent asks at the meeting where their phone number goes. The chair of the association shows the "Privacy" page and the date of 31 July.
2. In March a family moves away and asks to be deleted. The request goes to the association, which removes the child from the list.
3. On 1 August a member of the association opens the admin screen to prepare the new year and finds the system empty of personal data.

## What the association gains

- A fixed deletion date that depends on nobody's memory
- Only the data the meal service needs
- Separate roles, so the kitchen does not see phone numbers
- A ready answer when a parent asks what happens to their details

## What the association does

1. Read the privacy policy on the page and make sure it names the right controller.
2. Tell parents at the first meeting what is kept and that it is deleted on 31 July.
3. Give a link and a PIN only to those with a role: admin, office, kitchen.
4. Delete the old spreadsheets you kept before the system.
5. In September, import a new list and send new links.
