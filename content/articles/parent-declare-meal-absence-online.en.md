**Short answer:** A parent reports it online in [All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/), the NexAIpla system for all-day primary schools. On the school's page they type their mobile number under "Will not eat today" and press one button, or they open the child's personal link from the SMS and tap "Not taking part in today's meal". The kitchen's portion count changes at once. No account and no app. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

At 07:20 a child wakes up with a temperature. The parent now has three jobs: call work, call the grandparents, and tell the school that one lunch is not needed. The third is the one that gets forgotten, because the office line is engaged and the parents' group chat already has forty unread messages. By midday a portion has been cooked for a child who is asleep on the sofa.

## Two ways to say it, both under a minute

The first needs only a phone number. On the school's home page, in the part for parents, there is a form called "Will not eat today". The parent types the mobile number the school has on record and presses "Register absence". If two or more children are listed under that number, the form asks which of them it concerns and the parent selects one or all.

The second is the personal link. Every child has their own link, which the parent receives by SMS. Opening it shows the child's name and class and one button: "Not taking part in today's meal". There is no password. If the temperature turns out to be a false alarm and the child goes to school after all, the same screen has the opposite button, "Taking part in today's meal", and the absence is cancelled.

## What happens the moment the button is pressed

Nobody has to copy anything. The absence appears among the office's entries for today, and the kitchen's table for that class shows one more absence and one portion fewer. The cook does not receive a message to read. The number on the screen is simply different.

That is the whole point. The parent's tap and the cook's number are the same piece of information, with no person in between who can forget it.

## Absences that are known in advance

Not every absence is a surprise. The dentist next Tuesday, a family trip from Thursday to Monday, a week when the child leaves early. From the personal link the parent opens "Future absences", picks a "From" and a "To" date, and adds the period. It is saved automatically. Tapping a day in the list removes that day, and "Clear" starts again from nothing.

For the kitchen this is the difference between finding out at 07:45 and knowing a week earlier.

## If the parent cannot go online

Some parents will keep phoning, and that is fine. The school office has its own screen, where it finds the child by name, class, or phone number and registers the absence in the same list. The parent who phoned and the parent who tapped end up in the same count. See [How does the school office record a meal absence when a parent phones?](/en/articles/secretariat-school-meal-absences).

## Three mornings

1. A mother with two children at the same school types her mobile number at 07:25, selects only the younger one, and registers the absence before she has finished her coffee.
2. A father opens the personal link on Sunday evening and enters Wednesday to Friday as future absences because of a family trip.
3. A child reported absent at 07:30 feels better by 08:00. The parent taps "Taking part in today's meal" and the portion returns to the count.

## What changes for parents and school

- The office phone is not the only way in at 07:30
- The kitchen's portions change without anyone retyping a message
- Absences for the coming days are entered once, in advance
- A mistake is corrected with one tap by the parent

## What it is not

It is a system for organising meals in an all-day school: who eats today, how many portions, which allergies. It is not a shop. Parents do not choose dishes from it and do not buy anything through it. The menu is the school's monthly menu, published for everyone to read.

## How a school introduces it to parents

1. Load the list of children with their parents' mobile numbers.
2. Send every family its personal link by SMS from the admin screen.
3. Announce one sentence on the school page and at the parents' meeting: "If your child will not eat, tell us from your phone."
4. In the first weeks the office enters on its own screen the absences that arrive by phone, so the count stays complete.
5. Remind parents of "Future absences" before holidays and long weekends.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does a parent report that the child will not eat today?

In [All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/), either by typing their mobile number into the "Will not eat today" form on the school page or by tapping the button on the child's personal link. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

### Does the parent need a password or an app?

No. The form needs only the mobile number the school has on record, and the personal link opens straight from the SMS in any browser. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

### Can the parent change their mind?

Yes. The personal link has a button that puts the child back into today's meal, and the kitchen count follows. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

### What if the parent has lost the link?

On the school page they choose "I lost my personal link", type their mobile number, and receive it again by SMS. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).
