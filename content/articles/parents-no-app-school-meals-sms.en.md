> **Quick answer:** No. In [All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/) parents install nothing and create no account. Each family receives by SMS one personal link per child and opens it in the phone's browser. From there they report absences, plan later ones, and update allergies. Anyone who loses the link gets it again by SMS by typing their mobile number. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

A school does not choose its users. It has three hundred families with every kind of phone, parents who work shifts, grandparents who take over on Wednesdays, families who arrived in the country last year. Anything that asks for a download, a sign-up by email, and a password with a capital and a symbol will be completed by half of them. The other half will keep phoning, and the system will show half a picture.

## What the parent receives

One SMS with one link. The link is personal and corresponds to one child. The parent taps it, the browser opens, and they see the child's name and class. There is no login screen in between.

That page holds three things. The button for today, "Not taking part in today's meal", with its opposite in case something changes. "Future absences", with a "From" and a "To" date. And the field "Allergies / foods to avoid", which is saved and reaches the kitchen screen.

A family with three children has three links. It sounds like a lot, but in practice the parent saves them once, as bookmarks or on the home screen, under each child's name.

## And without the link

A parent who cannot find the SMS at 07:25 is not locked out. On the school's home page the form "Will not eat today" asks only for the mobile number. If the number corresponds to more than one child, the parent chooses which the absence concerns.

To get the link back, they press "I lost my personal link", type their mobile number, and receive it again by SMS. They do not have to wait for someone at the association to send it again by hand.

## How the links reach everyone

The association imports the list of children with the parents' mobile numbers and presses "Send personal link" on the admin screen. The SMS goes to every registered mobile. A single link can also be copied for one-off sending, and there is a CSV export for anyone who prefers to send the messages another way.

Mobile numbers are checked before they are stored: eight-digit Cyprus numbers are accepted. A wrong number in the list means a family without a link, so the list is worth checking before sending.

## No password does not mean no protection

The link cannot be guessed. It is long and random, and it is renewed when it is sent again or recovered. It expires on 31 July, with the school year. The public forms limit the number of attempts, and their replies do not reveal whether a number is on the school's list.

The parent should treat the link like a key. They do not forward it to the class group. If they change their mobile number, they tell the association, which corrects the record and sends a new link.

The staff screens are a different matter. The office, the kitchen, and the admin role enter with a secret link and a PIN. The parent has no PIN, because they see only their own child.

## Three families

1. A grandmother who looks after her grandchild two days a week has the link saved on her home screen under the child's name. She presses one button and it is done.
2. A father changed phones and lost his messages. He presses "I lost my personal link", types his number, and has it again within a minute.
3. A mother of twins cannot remember which link is whose. She uses the form with her mobile number and picks the right child from the list.

## What the school gains

- No parent gets stuck on a sign-up, a password, or an app store
- The office does no password resets
- Link recovery is done by the parent, by SMS
- More families inside the system means more accurate portions

## How the association starts

1. Collect the list with name, class, and parent's mobile number for every child.
2. Check that the mobile numbers are correct eight-digit numbers.
3. Import the list and press "Send personal link".
4. Write an announcement on the home page explaining in two lines what the SMS they received is.
5. In the first week, show anyone who asks the form with the mobile number and the link recovery.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do parents need an app or an account?

No. In [All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/) the parent opens a personal link from an SMS in the phone's browser, with no sign-up and no password. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

Yes. The link is per child. A parent with several children can alternatively report an absence with their mobile number and select the child. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).
