> **Quick answer:** By dropping the files into the intake folder of [DocsApp](https://www.nexaipla.com/docsapp/). The application reads Word, Excel, and CSV files, along with PDF, text, and images, and indexes them on your disk. Then you ask in plain language from the browser, and Ollama's local models answer with a citation to the file. Neither a connection nor an account with a cloud service is needed. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

Most of a business's documents are not PDFs. They are Word files that are still changing: quotations, memos, minutes, procedures. And spreadsheets: price lists, budgets, lists of materials. They are the files of the work, not of the archive.

## The obvious solution and its price

The big office suites now offer an AI assistant inside the program. You press a button and question the document. It is convenient.

But it has three preconditions. A subscription per user, every month. An account with the provider, with your files in its cloud. And an internet connection every time you ask, because the model runs on its servers.

For many businesses those are acceptable. For an office with confidential files, for an engineer on a site with no signal, for someone who does not want another monthly charge, they are not.

## The other route

The application does not live inside Word or Excel. It is a separate program that reads their files.

You copy or drag the files into the intake folder. The application extracts the text from the Word document and the data from the spreadsheet, and builds the index on your disk.

You open the chat page at 127.0.0.1:8080 and ask. That address is your computer itself, not a website.

The office program does not even need to be open.

## What you ask a Word file

Whatever you would ask somebody who read it carefully.

"What are the contractor's obligations according to the quotation?"

"What was decided about the timetable in the minutes of the last meeting?"

"Which steps does the procedure describe for returning a product?"

There is also a one-click summary, for the document you have no time to read before the meeting.

## What you ask an Excel file

Here the question is usually a calculation.

"What is the total of the 'Cost' column for the 'Electrical' category?"

"Which materials have a quantity under ten?"

"Show the budget by phase as a chart."

The application produces charts and analysis from Excel and CSV, without your writing a formula.

## Questions that combine the two

It gets interesting when the files are in the same folder. The quotation is in Word, the detailed budget in Excel, the contract in PDF. All three concern the same project.

You ask the contract about the delivery deadline. You ask the quotation what is included. You ask the sheet how much the delayed phase costs. All in the same conversation, which is saved as a thread for you to continue tomorrow.

## Without the internet, literally

After installation, the application needs no connection. That is not only a matter of privacy. It is practical too.

On the building site, where the signal drops behind the concrete. On the plane, with the client's file on the laptop. In the village where the line cuts out when it rains. The program answers all the same.

## The file that changes

Word and Excel files are alive. Monday's quotation is not Thursday's quotation.

The application answers from the copy you gave it. If the original has changed, drop the new version into the intake folder. And keep a simple naming rule, for example a date in the name, so that the citation shows which version was read.

The application reads files; it does not edit them. It will change nothing in your Word file and will write to no Excel cell. That is reassuring: whatever you ask, the original stays as it was.

## What helps quality

In Word documents, real headings. A document with section titles is split into pieces that make sense.

In spreadsheets, one row of headings and one table per sheet. Merged cells and decorative blank rows make reading harder.

In both, file names that say what they contain. "Quotation_Municipality_2025-03.docx" helps both you and the citation. "New document (3).docx" helps nobody.

## What to expect of speed

The models run on your computer's processor. No graphics card is needed, but answers are not instant. For a person who would otherwise open six files to find one number, the wait is short.
