How much does AI automation really cost a small business?

For most small businesses, automation cost is **not** a single software subscription. It is the sum of setup (or configuration), ongoing fees, integration work, training, and the labour you still need for exceptions. Whether it pays off depends on how many hours or errors you remove from **one** clear process — not on buying “AI” as a label.

> **Quick answer:** Budget for **implementation**, **monthly operating cost**, **integration and training**, and **human review time**. Compare that total to **hours saved** and **revenue you can measure** (e.g. fewer missed enquiries). Use an illustrative model with your own numbers — NexAIpla does not publish fixed project prices without scope.

## What counts in the total cost

| Cost area | What to include |
|-----------|------------------|
| Implementation | Configuration, custom development, or rollout of a ready-made app |
| Software & AI usage | Subscriptions, API usage, hosting |
| Integration | Links to calendar, accounting, CRM, or your website |
| Training & change | Time for staff to learn and adjust the process |
| Ongoing review | Someone still checks exceptions, quality, and edge cases |

Saving employee time does **not** always equal cash in the bank — you may reinvest time into sales or service unless you measure it.

## Illustrative 12-month model (not a quote)

| One-time setup | €2,000 |

**Savings side (same example):**

| Line | Illustrative amount |
|------|---------------------|
| Hours saved per week | 8 |
| Loaded labour value used in the model | €18/hour (your choice) |
| Annual labour value in the model | 8 × 52 × €18 ≈ €7,488 |
| Recoverable missed enquiries (if measured) | €0 in this example — add only if you track it |

**Net in the model:** €7,488 − €3,840 ≈ **€3,648** year 1 (before tax, before reinvested time).

Payback in this illustration: roughly **6–7 months**. Your real payback depends on adoption, process fit, and whether saved time is redeployed or lost.

## How to calculate ROI without fantasy numbers

1. Pick **one process** (booking, inbox triage, document intake, etc.).
2. Measure **baseline**: hours/week, error rate, or missed enquiries for 2–4 weeks.
3. List **all costs** (table above) from quotes or vendor pages — not guesses about NexAIpla pricing.
4. Re-measure after a **pilot** (30–60 days).
5. ROI formula (simplified):

```text
Annual benefit ≈ (hours saved × hourly value you assign) + measured extra revenue − extra running cost
Payback months ≈ total first-year cost ÷ (monthly benefit − monthly running cost)
```

Label every figure **illustrative** until you have invoices or a signed proposal.

## When automation is cheap vs expensive

**Often lower cost:** One ready-made workflow (online booking, structured FAQs, simple document Q&A) on a process you already do digitally.

**Often higher cost:** Multiple integrations, legacy software, heavy compliance, or custom AI across many departments.

**Poor ROI:** Automating a broken process, or buying tools nobody uses.

## Limitations and alternatives

- Spreadsheets + clear checklists may be enough for very low volume.
- Hiring part-time admin can beat custom software for rare tasks.
- Do not automate solely because competitors mention AI — fix the process first.
