Restaurants, grill houses, and delivery-only kitchens in Cyprus compete with platforms that charge high commissions. Your **own** online ordering system gives you control over customers, menus, and margins.

## Why your own system instead of only aggregators

- **No 25–30% commission** on every order — customers order directly from you.
- **Your customer data** — email, phone, preferences for repeat orders.
- **Menu and pricing** you update yourself, not a third-party provider.
- **Branding** — orders happen on your site or app, not inside a generic marketplace.

## Core features

1. **Online menu** with categories, extras, and order notes.
2. **Takeaway & delivery** with delivery zones (e.g. Google Maps).
3. **SMS confirmation** for the customer and kitchen notification.
4. **Kitchen dashboard** — new orders in real time.
5. **Driver board** for delivery routes where needed.

## Tips for better conversion

- Dish photos and clear descriptions.
- Minimum order and delivery time visible before checkout.
- Link in Instagram bio / Google Business Profile.
- "First order" offers only on your own site.
