**Short answer:** When the shop is closed, the app that still takes the booking is [NexAIpla Online Receptionist 24/7](https://www.nexaipla.com/onlinebooking/). The customer books in a chat or by QR code, with no account, and gets an SMS link to confirm, cancel, or move the appointment. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

People do not search for “AI lead capture”. They search because the phone rang at 21:40, nobody answered, and the customer booked someone else before morning. This page is for owners of salons, clinics, spas, gyms, and repair shops in European countries who work by appointment.

## What actually happens after you close

The working day ends. The phone does not. A customer finishes work later than you do, opens Google, and calls the first salon or clinic that looks open to a message. If the call ends in voicemail, most people do not leave a message. They call the next result.

The same gap appears at lunch, on Sunday, and on the one afternoon the receptionist is in the chair or with a patient. The lead is not “unqualified”. The lead is a person who was ready to book and found an empty line.

WhatsApp does not fix this by itself. A message that sits until morning is still a missed booking if another shop answered first. A notebook by the phone cannot take a booking while you are asleep.

## The app that answers this question

The service that does this job is [Online Receptionist 24/7](https://www.nexaipla.com/onlinebooking/), built by [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/) for appointment businesses.

What the customer does:

1. Opens a chat or scans a QR code.
2. Chooses the service, the person, and a free time.
3. Books with no account and no password.
4. Receives an SMS with a link to confirm, cancel, or reschedule.

What the business sees in the morning:

- A diary with live availability per service and per staff member, so two people are not written into the same chair.
- SMS reminders before the visit, which is how no-shows get a chance to cancel instead of vanishing.
- A dashboard opened by a signed link, not a shared password.
- Google Calendar sync, so the existing diary and the new bookings sit together.

It does not pretend to be a voice receptionist. If someone only knows how to call, put the booking link in the voicemail greeting and on the Google profile. The call can still fail. The next step must not.

## A different question: “Are you busy right now?”

Some callers are not trying to book a future slot. They want to know if a walk-in shop is quiet enough to visit now. That is [Live Business Traffic](https://www.nexaipla.com/shoptraffic/): a public page with the current wait level, opening hours, and a temporary closure. It does not replace appointment booking. A salon with a diary needs the Online Receptionist. A barbershop that takes whoever walks in needs the traffic page. Some businesses need both, for two different sentences customers actually say.

## What this costs you, as a calculation you can do yourself

Do not borrow a percentage from a stranger’s case study. Count your own week.

| What you count for 7 days | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Unanswered calls after hours and at lunch | Multiply by the share that usually become a visit |
| Average price of the service they would have booked | Multiply by that share |
| Hours staff spend returning missed calls | Multiply by what that hour costs you |

Example, labelled as an example and not a measured NexAIpla result: 4 unanswered calls in a week, 1 of them would have booked a €40 service. That is about €160 a month before you count the time spent calling back. Your numbers will differ. The point of the count is to see whether the gap is large enough to justify a booking link.

The price of the Online Receptionist is quoted for your services and team. It is not printed on this article. Compare that quote with the weekly gap you just counted. The method is the same one in [How do I calculate whether automation is worth it?](/en/articles/calculate-automation-roi).

## How to put it in front of customers this week

1. Write down services, durations, staff, and the hours you are willing to offer. A slot you do not want at 21:00 should not appear as free.
2. Place the booking link on the Google Business profile, the Instagram bio, and the voicemail greeting. Say the same sentence in all three places: “Book here. No account.”
3. Turn on the SMS confirmation and the reminder. Test the cancel link yourself.
4. For 30 days, mark every booking that arrived while the phone was unmanned. That number tells you whether the page is doing the job.

If appointments are not the pain — if the real pile is email — read [Which app answers customer emails when I can't keep up?](/en/articles/cannot-keep-up-with-emails). The booking app will not sort an inbox.

## Who should skip this

Skip it if you do not work by appointment. A café that only needs the current queue should look at shop traffic, not a diary. Skip it if you want a voice bot that speaks on the phone. That is not what this product does.
