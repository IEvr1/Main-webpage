> **Quick answer:** Parents see it on the school's home page in [All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/). The parents' association uploads a photo or scan of the monthly meal menu, chooses the month and the year, and the menu is visible to everyone. Announcements and useful phone numbers sit under it. No login is needed, and the page exists in Greek and in English. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

The month's menu begins its life as paper. It is printed, it goes into three hundred school bags, and a week later half the copies are under a car seat. Somebody photographs it and sends it to the parents' group, where by Wednesday it is covered by a hundred messages about a lost cardigan. Then the questions to the school office begin: "What is it tomorrow?"

## One place that does not get buried

The school's home page has a fixed section headed "Monthly meal menu". It is the first thing anyone sees on opening the page. It does not scroll away like a message, it does not depend on whether the child remembered to hand over the paper, and it is the same for everybody.

Nobody needs a personal link to see it. The grandmother who collects the child every Thursday, the parent who does not live in the same house, the new family that enrolled in November all open the same address.

## How it goes up

On the admin screen the association selects the photo or scan of the menu, sets the year and the month, and presses "Save menu". The home page is updated for everyone. When the month changes, the next one goes up.

It is deliberate that this is an image and not a table to be typed. Somebody already produces the menu, by hand or in a document, and there is no reason to write it out again dish by dish in a form. One photo, three taps, and the job is finished.

The image does have to be readable on a phone screen. Photograph the sheet flat, in good light and without a shadow, or scan it. If parents have to zoom in and guess, they will phone anyway.

## The announcements underneath

Menus change. The supplier did not bring fish, Friday is a holiday, one day lunch will be cold because of a trip. Those do not need a new menu. They need an announcement. In the admin screen you write a "New announcement", mark it as active, and save. Whatever is active shows under the menu. Whatever has gone stale is removed.

Further down are the useful phone numbers, one line each. On a phone, one touch dials.

## What a parent does with the menu

They plan the evening meal, so that the child does not eat lentils twice in one day. They see in advance a day with food the child must not eat, and they update the allergies on the personal link or speak to the school. They know what to answer when the child says there was "nothing today".

What they do not do is choose. The menu is not a list to pick from. The child eats that day's all-day school meal, and the system is concerned with who will be present, not with what each person selects.

## Why this cuts phone calls

The question "what is it tomorrow?" is not important, but it is frequent. Every such call holds the line while someone else is trying to say that their child is ill. Once the menu has a permanent address, the office answers once, with the link, and does not hear the question from the same parent again.

## How it is used during the month

1. On the last Friday of the month the association photographs next month's menu and uploads it. On Monday morning everyone sees it, with no paper handed out.
2. On Tuesday the fish is replaced by chicken. An active announcement says so, and on Wednesday it is removed.
3. The mother of a child with an intolerance sees pasta with yoghurt on Thursday and notes it in the allergies on the personal link, so that the kitchen sees it.

## What changes

- One menu, one address, the same for every family
- No retyping: a photo is enough
- Changes are written as an announcement beside the menu
- Fewer "what is it tomorrow?" calls to the school office

## Every month

1. Prepare the menu as you always do, and photograph or scan it clearly.
2. Open the admin screen with the association's link and PIN.
3. Select the image, set the year and the month, and save.
4. Open the home page on a phone and make sure it can be read without effort.
5. For every change during the month, write an active announcement and delete it once it has passed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do parents see the monthly meal menu?

On the school's home page in [All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/), in the "Monthly meal menu" section, without logging in anywhere. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

### Do we have to type the menu dish by dish?

No. You upload a photo or a scan of the menu and set the year and the month. The image only needs to be clearly readable on a phone. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

### How do we announce a change to one day's food?

With an active announcement from the admin screen. It appears under the menu and is removed when it is no longer needed. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

### Can parents choose food from the menu?

No. The menu is for information. The system organises absences, portions, and allergies, and it does not take dish selections. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).
