All-day primary schools in European countries still collect meal absences by phone and paper in the morning. A meal system replaces that with a monthly menu, an SMS when a child will not eat, and a live portion count for the kitchen.

## What the school needs

1. Parents mark an absence without an app account.
2. The secretariat sees the day in one list.
3. The kitchen sees how many portions to prepare.
