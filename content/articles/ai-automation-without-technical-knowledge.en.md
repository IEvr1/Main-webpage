How can I use AI in my small business without technical knowledge?

You do not need to code, pick models, or run a “digital transformation” programme. For most small businesses, **using AI** means automating **one repeated task** with a ready-made app or a scoped custom tool — starting from the work you already describe in plain language.

> **Quick answer:** Pick **one painful, repetitive workflow** (messages, bookings, documents, data copying). Check [ready-made NexAIpla apps](https://www.nexaipla.com/onlinebooking/) for a fit. If none fit, use [Custom AI tools](https://www.nexaipla.com/ai/) for a **48-hour feasibility reply**. ## You already have the hard part: process knowledge

You know which customer changes their mind on Fridays, which spreadsheet breaks, which message gets copy-pasted ten times a day. **That** is what automation needs — not GPU specs.

Describe the job like training a new employee:

- What arrives (email, phone note, form).
- What you do with it.
- Where it lands (calendar, invoice, warehouse slip).
- How long it takes and where mistakes happen.

No acronyms required.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use AI if I am not technical?

Yes. You describe the work; tools or a feasibility reply translate it into a solution scope.

### Should I start with ChatGPT for everything?

Only for drafts or exploration. Running the business still needs **one workflow** connected to your channels — score + pilot first.

### How long until something works?

Readiness score: same day. First automation pilot: often **2–6 weeks** depending on process and app fit.

### Do ready-made apps need IT staff?

Usually no — configuration and training, not coding. Custom builds include handover in plain language.
