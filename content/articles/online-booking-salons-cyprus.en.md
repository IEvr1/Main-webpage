Salons, spas, clinics, and medical practices in Cyprus lose time and revenue when clients call outside business hours, forget appointments, or simply don't show up. An **online booking system** solves these problems without requiring a 24/7 reception desk.

## Why you need online booking

- **24/7 availability** — clients book when it suits them, including evenings and weekends.
- **Fewer no-shows** — automatic SMS reminders before appointments cut last-minute cancellations.
- **Fewer phone interruptions** — staff focus on service instead of answering availability calls.
- **A fuller calendar** — empty slots fill faster when booking is one click away.

## What to look for in a solution

1. **Chat or booking form** showing real available time slots.
2. **SMS confirmation** immediately after booking.
3. **Reminder** 24 (or 48) hours before the appointment.
4. **Staff dashboard** — no technical skills required.
5. **QR code** in-store so walk-in clients can easily book their next visit.

## Who it's for

Online booking works especially well for hair salons, barbers, aesthetic clinics, physiotherapy, dental practices, gyms with personal training, auto repair shops, and any business that runs on **scheduled appointments**.
