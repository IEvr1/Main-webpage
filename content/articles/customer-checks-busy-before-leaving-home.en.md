**Short answer:** You know before you leave home when the shop has a [Live Business Traffic](https://www.nexaipla.com/shoptraffic/) page. You open the shop's link on your phone and see a colour-coded busy level, whether it is open now, and its hours. No account and no app. If the shop you go to does not have such a page yet, the owner can ask NexAIpla for one. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

You need a haircut, a prescription, a coffee with somewhere to sit. The shop is ten minutes away. Whether those ten minutes are well spent depends on something you cannot see: how many people got there before you. Map apps sometimes show a "popular times" chart, but that is an average of past weeks and not a statement about this afternoon.

## What the page tells you

A shop's page in [Live Business Traffic](https://www.nexaipla.com/shoptraffic/) shows a level set by the people who are inside the shop at this moment. There are four levels, from LOW in green to VERY BUSY in red. It is not a forecast. It is what the person behind the counter can see.

Beside the level you read whether the shop is open now, the weekly hours, and any announcement. The announcement deserves a glance, because that is where the unusual things are written: "back in ten minutes", "closing at 14:00 today".

What each colour means exactly is decided by each shop. In a barbershop MEDIUM may be one person ahead of you, in a pharmacy three. After a visit or two you know what to expect from the amber of your own shop.

## How to use it well

Save it. On phones that support it, the page goes on the home screen like an icon, and you do not have to hunt for the link every time.

Look twice. Once when you start thinking of going, and once just before you walk out of the door. If you leave the page open it refreshes by itself and you watch the level change.

Allow for the journey. If you are twenty minutes away, the level may change before you arrive. And a BUSY half an hour before closing time means you may not be served at all.

## What it will not do for you

The page does not hold a place and does not put you in a queue. It gives no minutes of waiting and sends you no notification. When you arrive, it is first come, first served, as always. And because the level is tapped by a person who is working at the same time, in a sudden rush it may lag by a few minutes. It is an honest picture, not a guarantee.

If you need a set time with a particular person, you are looking for a booking and not for a busy level. Businesses that work by appointment use [Online Receptionist 24/7](https://www.nexaipla.com/onlinebooking/) for that.

## If you are the owner reading this

Your customer asks this question every time; you simply do not hear it. They answer it themselves, usually pessimistically, and go elsewhere or come along with everyone else. A busy page gives them a reason to choose you and, better still, to choose your quiet hour.

The page does not ask you to change the way you work. You take no bookings, hand out no numbers, and keep nobody's details. You simply say what you already see from the counter, with one tap, and let the customer decide which hour suits them.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know if a shop is busy before I leave home?

You open the shop's page in [Live Business Traffic](https://www.nexaipla.com/shoptraffic/). It shows a live colour-coded level set by the staff, whether the shop is open, and the hours. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

### Is it the same as "popular times" on a map?

No. Popular times are averages from earlier visits. This level is set today, by the people inside the shop, and it changes during the day. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

### Do I have to give a phone number or sign up anywhere?

No. The page is public and anonymous. You give nothing and you install nothing. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).
