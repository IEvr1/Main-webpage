The double booking is the most embarrassing mistake for a small business, because it happens in front of two people at once. And it is almost never one person's carelessness. The fault is that appointments are written in more than one place.

## Where double bookings come from

Count the ways an appointment gets made in your shop. The phone. Messages on social media. The owner's personal mobile. The customer who asks on the way out. The employee who says "yes, come" to an acquaintance.

Each way has its own place of record. The notebook, a message, memory. The notebook does not know what the mobile promised.

There is also time. The eleven-at-night message will be written in the notebook in the morning. Meanwhile, another customer may have asked for the same time through another channel.

## The second kind: the overlap

Two appointments need not start at the same time to collide. A colour that starts at four lasts until six. A haircut written in at five falls in the middle of it.

In the notebook that shows only if someone has drawn a line all the way down. In a hurry they do not draw it.

## How live availability works

First the customer picks what they want done and by whom. The system knows how long the service lasts and which appointments that person already has. It calculates at that moment which times fit and shows only those.

It does not show a list that was made in the morning. It calculates it each time someone asks.

When one customer books five o'clock, the next one to open the page does not see five o'clock. Nor half past five, if the first one's service lasts an hour.

## Two customers at the same moment

What if two people are looking at the same free time simultaneously? One will tap first. The time becomes theirs. The other learns that it is no longer available and picks another.

Neither of them received a confirmation SMS for a time they do not have.

## Per person, not per shop

Availability is calculated for each team member separately. Five o'clock may be booked for Maria and free for Eleni. The customer with no preference finds a time. The one who wants Maria sees when she can.

That also solves a notebook error that is not often mentioned: the appointment written at the right time but in the wrong column.

## The rule that makes the system work

The system protects only the appointments that pass through it. If you go on taking bookings on the phone and writing them on paper, you have two sources again, and the double bookings will be back.

There is one rule: every appointment comes in through the same door. When somebody phones, whoever answers opens the booking page and makes the appointment on their behalf, with the customer's mobile number. The customer gets their SMS like everyone else.

The same goes for messages on social media. The reply is the link: "Book here; you can see the free times."

For the first two weeks that takes discipline. After that it becomes habit, and the notebook stays in the drawer.

## Leave, days off, and emergencies

An appointment made for a time when the person will not be there is a double booking too. Leave and days off are declared as closures on the board, and availability respects them.

If someone falls ill in the morning with six appointments, the board has an emergency reschedule. You do not have to phone six people with the notebook in your hand.

## The customer who changes

When a customer changes the time from the SMS link, the old time is freed and the new one is reserved in the same move. There is no moment at which they hold both, nor one at which they hold neither.

In the notebook, a change is two actions: erase here, write there. The first is often forgotten.

## What it cannot prevent

The appointment you gave by word of mouth and never entered. The customer who came without an appointment and whom you fitted "in between". Those are human decisions outside the system, and the system does not see them.

The solution is not technical. It is for the whole team to have the same answer when someone asks "have you a gap?": "Let us look at the page together."

## Three collisions that do not happen

1. Two customers want Saturday at eleven with the same hairdresser. The first books. The second sees only half past twelve and later.
2. A two-hour colour from four. Nobody can book a haircut at five with the same person, because the time is not offered.
3. A customer phones. Reception opens the page, books on his behalf, and the appointment goes into the same source as the rest.

## What the business gains

- A time that has been booked is not offered again
- Durations are taken into account, so there are no overlaps
- Each person has their own availability
- Changes free and reserve in the same move

## How you stop double bookings

1. Write down all the ways appointments are made today.
2. Set a correct duration for each service.
3. Decide that all appointments pass through the booking page, phone ones included.
4. Enter the month's leave and days off on the board.
5. Retire the notebook on the day you start, not gradually.
