**Short answer:** The office records it on the secretariat screen of [All-day School — Meals](https://www.nexaipla.com/schoolmeals/). It searches for the child by name, class, or phone number, presses "Register absence", and the absence joins the same list that parents fill in from their phones. "Today's entries" shows the whole day and lets a mistake be removed. The kitchen's portions update at once. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://www.nexaipla.com/).

However well a system is set up for parents, the office phone will ring in the morning. Someone is driving, someone has no data on their phone, someone simply prefers to tell a person. The aim is not to stop those calls. It is to make sure that what was said in their thirty seconds is not lost.

## The slip of paper that never arrives

The usual scene is familiar. The secretary picks up, hears "Michalis in D2 will not eat today", writes it on a pad, and takes the next line. By 08:30 the pad has eight names, two without a class and one that cannot be read. Someone has to carry it to the kitchen or dictate it over the internal phone. If a parent walks into the office at that moment, the pad waits.

The information exists. It is simply in the wrong place, in the wrong form.

## Search, one button, done

The secretariat screen has a search field. The secretary types whatever she heard: part of the name, the class, or the phone number. The child appears, with the "Register absence" button beside it. One press and the absence is entered.

The three ways of searching are not there by accident. The name is the obvious one, but it confuses when the school has three boys called Giorgos. The class helps when a parent says "my son in B1" before giving a name. And the phone number is the fastest of all: if the office handset shows the calling number, the secretary types it and sees that family's children before the parent has finished the sentence.

## The day's entries

Under the search are "Today's entries". This is the complete picture of the day. If a parent rings back to ask whether "it was entered", the answer is on the screen.

Mistakes are corrected there. If the wrong Giorgos was entered, the "Remove" button beside the entry deletes it and the portion returns to the count. No call to the kitchen is needed to say "forget what I told you earlier".

## Why the kitchen no longer waits for the office

An absence registered by the office is the same as one registered by a parent. It goes into the same sum and takes one portion off the child's class in the kitchen table. Nobody carries anything. The secretary is no longer the courier between the telephone and the pot.

The same holds when the information starts inside the school. A teacher reports at 09:15 that a child has left with a parent after falling ill. The office enters it, and if the kitchen has not yet fixed its quantities, the number is already right.

## How the office gets in

The screen does not open from an ordinary public link. The office has its own secret link and the PIN of its role. It opens the link, enters the PIN, presses "Enter", and the session continues without the link's key showing in the address bar. Both are needed to get in.

The role is limited to what the office does: searching for children and the day's absences. Importing the list, sending links to parents, the menu, and the announcements belong to the association's admin role. The kitchen has a third role of its own.

## Three phone calls

1. 07:35, a mother calls from the car. The secretary types the last four digits she sees on the handset, two siblings appear, and she registers an absence for one of them.
2. 07:50, a grandfather says only "my granddaughter, Eleni, in Year 3". A search by class, two Elenis in the list, one question about the surname, one button.
3. 08:10, a father rings back: the child is coming after all. The secretary finds the entry in "Today's entries" and presses "Remove".

## What changes in the office

- A phoned absence is entered while the parent is still on the line
- No pad that has to be carried to the kitchen
- Mistakes are seen and removed on the same screen
- "Did you enter it?" is answered at a glance

## How the office fits it into the morning

1. Open the office link on the office computer and enter the PIN before the calls begin.
2. Keep the screen open beside the telephone.
3. On every call, search with whatever you hear first: name, class, or number.
4. Confirm name and class aloud before you press the button.
5. When the rush is over, look through "Today's entries" for double or wrong entries.
