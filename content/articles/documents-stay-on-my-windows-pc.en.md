> **Quick answer:** On the disk of the computer where you installed [DocsApp](https://www.nexaipla.com/docsapp/), and nowhere else. The files you drop into the intake folder, the index the application builds, and the saved conversations are all local. The AI models run on the same machine through Ollama. There is no account, no sync, and no NexAIpla server that keeps a copy. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

The question "where is my data" usually has an answer with many words: regions, encryption, certifications. Here the answer is one disk. You can point to it with a finger.

## Four things that are stored

The documents themselves. The files you drop into the intake folder: PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text, images. They stay as files on the disk.

The index. To find the relevant passages quickly, the application splits documents into pieces and computes a numerical representation of each. That index is also a file on your disk. It contains excerpts of the documents, so it is just as sensitive as they are.

The conversations. The questions you asked and the answers you got are saved as threads, so that you can find them again. Those are local too.

The models. The model files Ollama downloaded at installation. They contain nothing of yours: they are the "brain", not the memory.

## What does not exist

There is no user account with a NexAIpla service. There is no cloud sync. There is no call to an outside model when you ask a question. There is no telemetry sending the content of your documents.

The interface opens at the address 127.0.0.1:8080, which is your computer itself. The browser does not go out to the internet to show the page.

## How you verify it yourself

Do not believe it because we write it. Check it.

Pull out the network cable and switch off the Wi-Fi. Ask a question. If the answer comes, the computation happened on your machine.

Or ask your technician to watch the network connections while you are asking. They will see traffic only to the computer itself.

## Who can see them

Whoever can see your disk. That is the price and the advantage of local: the security is that of your computer, no more and no less.

If the computer is shared and has no password, the documents are accessible to whoever sits in front of it. If it is locked, with a separate Windows account for each user and an encrypted disk, they are as safe as anything else you keep there.

The application does not do those things. You or your technician do them, and they are worth doing in any case.

## Four settings worth having

A password on the Windows account and automatic screen lock after a few minutes.

Disk encryption. Windows offers it in the professional editions. If the laptop is stolen, the disk cannot be read.

A backup. "Only on your disk" also means that, if the disk fails, the application has nowhere to bring things back from. Keep a copy of the originals on a second medium that you control.

One computer with a named person in charge. In an office of five, decide which machine has the application and who sits there.

## The automatic cleanup

The application has automatic cleanup after six months. The reasoning is simple: what is not needed should not remain. An index with excerpts from files closed two years ago is risk without benefit.

That means the application is not your archive. Originals that you must keep for years stay in your normal filing system. DocsApp is the tool you question them with while you are working on them.

## What you tell your client

A client who asks "do you use AI on my documents?" deserves an exact answer. With a cloud tool the honest answer includes the name of a third company and a country.

Here the answer fits in one sentence: "Yes, with a program that runs on our office computer. Your documents are not sent anywhere."

## What does not hold

The application does not make your computer impregnable. If the machine has malware, or if somebody knows your password, the documents are at risk like any other file. "Local" takes the third party out of the equation. It does not take away the need for basic care of the computer.

## Three cases

1. An accounting office: the computer with the application is in the owner's room, with an encrypted disk. Client files do not leave the room.
2. A consultant with a laptop: she questions a client's documents on the train, with no Wi-Fi. If the laptop is lost, the encrypted disk does not open.
3. An office technician watches the network traffic during a question and sees only local communication.

## What you gain

- You know which disk each file is on
- There is no third company processing the documents
- You can prove it with the network disconnected
- Old data is cleaned up automatically after six months

## How you set it up responsibly

1. Choose the computer that will have the application and name a person in charge.
2. Turn on a password, automatic lock, and disk encryption.
3. Decide where the backups of the originals are kept.
4. Run the test with the network disconnected and record the result.
5. Write one sentence on how you explain it to clients.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where are my documents stored?

On the disk of the computer where [DocsApp](https://www.nexaipla.com/docsapp/) runs. The same holds for the index and the saved conversations. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

### Does NexAIpla keep a copy of the documents?

No. There is no account, sync, or server that receives your files or your questions. Homepage: [NexAIpla](https://nexaipla.com) (nexaipla.com).

### Does data stay in the application for ever?
