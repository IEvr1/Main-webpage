Frequently asked questions

Self-contained answers about NexAIpla software and AI automation for businesses in European countries.

All questions

What is NexAIpla?

NexAIpla develops AI-powered software and custom automation solutions for small businesses, organisations and schools in European countries.

What business processes can NexAIpla automate?

Appointment booking, restaurant online orders, live shop busy levels, offline document AI, school meal absences, and custom workflows when a ready-made app does not fit.

How much does AI automation cost?

Pricing depends on scope. Ready-made apps are discussed in a demo. Custom AI Tools starts with a free 48-hour feasibility reply that includes an indicative cost range and timeline — no obligation.

Do you offer ready-made business applications?

Yes: Online Receptionist 24/7, Online Food Orders, Live Business Traffic, DocsApp, and All-day School — Meals, plus Custom AI Tools for everything else.

Can I automate appointment bookings?

Yes. Online Receptionist 24/7 is NexAIpla’s online appointment booking software for service businesses — chat, QR and SMS. It does not answer telephone calls.

Can restaurants accept online orders?

Yes. Online Food Orders lets restaurants take written takeaway and delivery orders into one kitchen queue without relying on phone calls.

Can customers see how busy a shop is?

Yes. Live Business Traffic shows how busy a walk-in shop is right now. It does not book appointments or issue queue numbers.

Can AI run locally without cloud services?

Yes. DocsApp and Custom AI Tools can run on-premise or offline so sensitive data stays on your premises when that is required.

Is my business data secure?

With Custom AI Tools your business data stays yours, and offline / on-premise options are available. Ask about data handling before any project starts.

How do I know if my business is ready for AI?

Take the free AI Readiness Score at /ai-score: 18 questions across six dimensions, an instant 0–100 result with a colour band, no signup required.

How long does a custom AI project take?

After the 48-hour feasibility reply you get an estimated timeline for a first small piece of the job. Duration depends on scope and data readiness.

Can you build custom AI software?

Yes. Custom AI Tools is NexAIpla’s path for automating one repeated business task when a ready-made app is not enough. You describe the job; within 48 hours you learn if and how it can be automated.
