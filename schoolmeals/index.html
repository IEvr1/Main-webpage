All-day meal organisation — in real time

Monthly meal schedule, children's absences without phone calls, and live portion counts for the kitchen. For primary schools and parents' associations. For businesses in Cyprus and Greece.

  • Morning phone calls & paper notes Parents register absences online in seconds — no calls to the school office, no lost notes.
  • Kitchen doesn't know how many portions Live counts by class: enrolled, absences, and portions to prepare — updated in real time.
  • Allergy risks & missing information Allergies and notes visible to the kitchen for every child eating today — fewer mistakes, safer meals.
  • WhatsApp groups & manual spreadsheets One central system replaces scattered messages — menu, announcements, and absence lists in one place.

Frequently asked questions

Do parents need to register or download an app?

No. Parents receive a personal link via SMS and can declare absences from any browser — no accounts or app installation required.

How does the kitchen know how many meals to prepare?

The kitchen dashboard shows live counts by class: enrolled children, today's absences, and exact portions to prepare — with allergies listed for each child eating today.

Is it GDPR compliant?

Yes. Personal data is automatically purged on 31 July each year, staff access is PIN-protected, and a privacy policy is published on the site.

Can it be deployed for our school?

Yes. Each school gets its own deployment with full data isolation. Contact us to set up your school — import the roster, send parent links, and go live.
