Know how busy it is—before you leave.

Shows customers how busy the business is right now — and when you are open. Ideal for barbershops, salons, pharmacies, cafés, and other walk-in businesses. For businesses in European countries.

  • Customers don’t know how busy it is A public shop page with colour-coded wait levels — people see the real picture before they leave home.
  • Constant “are you free?” calls Customers check the link instead of calling — fewer interruptions while you serve walk-ins.
  • Unclear opening hours & closures Weekly hours, special closed days, and emergency close — shown clearly as open or temporarily closed.
  • Hard to update status while working One-tap owner buttons for LOW / MEDIUM / BUSY / VERY BUSY — update traffic between clients in seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Is this for walk-in businesses?

Yes. It is designed for shops without appointments — barbershops, salons, pharmacies, cafés, and similar walk-in businesses.

How do customers see traffic levels?

Each shop has a public page with colour-coded wait levels, opening hours, announcements, and automatic refresh — in Greek and English.

How does the owner update traffic?

Through a private owner screen with a personal link — four buttons for wait level, no shared password.

Does it replace appointment booking?

No. It answers a different question — "how busy is it right now?" rather than "what time is my appointment?". It can work alongside online booking.
